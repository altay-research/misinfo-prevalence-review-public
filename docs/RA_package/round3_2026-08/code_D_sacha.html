The task. Each card shows one published quantitative claim about the amount of misinformation, verbatim. Code what the NUMBER actually is, from the text alone.

The key question: if this number were 100%, what would that mean? A share of consumption = Exposure. A share of sampled items = Content (or the keyword-topical variant if the sample was built by keyword search and weighted by views). A share of people who say they encountered it = Recall. A share of activity from a top group = Concentration. A raw count with no denominator at all = Reach-absolute.

Blind rules. Don't consult the dataset. Unsure? Pick the closest and note your doubt.
