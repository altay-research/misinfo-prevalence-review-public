# Value verification package (built from estimates_v1.7.14_frozen.csv)

Open `code_V_laura.html` in a browser (keep the `papers/` folder next to it). One paper at a time:
the paper on the left, the estimates to verify on the right. Everything saves in the browser;
press "Download" whenever you stop and send the CSV.

Tiers: 1 = behavioural exposure, reach, concentration (40 papers, 114 estimates);
2 = sharing (23, 50); 3 = survey recall (26, 57).
Total 89 papers, 221 estimates. Papers without a PDF are shown as a text extraction.
The answer key is NOT in this folder.

**Instrument correction (2026-09-13, after scoring both coders).** The coder page defined SHARING as "share of shares, retweets or posts that carried misinformation, or share of users who shared it, from platform data". The second half contradicts the codebook: a share of PEOPLE who shared at least once is REACH (reach_subtype = sharing), and SHARING is reserved for shares of sharing acts. Eight of Laura's WRONG-CONSTRUCT verdicts follow the page's wording and are scored as an instrument inconsistency, not as coder or dataset error. The delivered coder files are unchanged.
