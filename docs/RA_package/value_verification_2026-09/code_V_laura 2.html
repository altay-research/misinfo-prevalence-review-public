Instructions (read once before starting)
The task. For each paper we list the estimates our pipeline extracted from it, described only by where they come from (country, platform, period, what is measured). The numbers themselves are hidden. For every listed estimate, find it in the paper and record: the value (as a percentage), what it is a share of (in your own words: "of all URLs visited by the panel", "of respondents", "of all retweets of political links"), the verbatim sentence or table cell it comes from, and which construct it is. If you cannot find any figure matching the description, choose NOT FOUND. If the paper gives several figures that fit the description equally well, choose AMBIGUOUS, record the one you would pick, and list the others in the note.

Also tell us what we missed. At the bottom of each paper there is a box for qualifying estimates we did not list: any share of a diet, of people, of content, of sharing, or of activity that concerns misinformation, disinformation, fake or false news, or unreliable sources, with a denominator.

Constructs (pick the one the number answers): EXPOSURE = share of what people actually consumed (visits, time, impressions) that was misinformation; REACH = share of people who encountered it at least once, from behavioural data; SHARING = share of shares, retweets or posts that carried misinformation, or share of users who shared it, from platform data; CONCENTRATION = share of all misinformation exposure or sharing accounted for by a top group of users or sources (record the group, e.g. "top 1% of users", in the note); RECALL = share of survey respondents who say they saw or shared it; CONTENT = share of items in a sample that were classified as misinformation; QUALITY = a quality or reliability rating, not a truth judgement; OTHER.

Order: papers are grouped in three tiers. Tier 1 (40 papers, 114 estimates) carries the paper's behavioural claims and comes first; tier 2 (23, 50) adds sharing; tier 3 (26, 57) adds surveys. Stop wherever your time runs out; every completed paper counts. Answers save automatically in this browser; download the CSV when you stop.
