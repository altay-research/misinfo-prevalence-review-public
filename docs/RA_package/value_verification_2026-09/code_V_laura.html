The task. For each paper we show every estimate our pipeline extracted from it: the value, the sentence or table cell it was taken from, what we recorded it as a share of, and the construct we filed it under. Check each one against the paper and pick a verdict: CORRECT (the number is in the paper, the sentence is real, and the denominator and construct are right); WRONG VALUE (the number or the sentence does not match the paper, or a different figure should have been used: give the right value and sentence); WRONG DENOMINATOR OR CONSTRUCT (the number is real but it is a share of something else, or it belongs to another construct: say which); CANNOT FIND (the sentence is not in the paper). Add a note whenever you hesitate.

Also tell us what we missed. At the bottom of each paper there is a box for qualifying estimates we did not list: any share of a diet, of people, of content, of sharing, or of activity that concerns misinformation, disinformation, fake or false news, or unreliable sources, with a denominator. This is the most valuable part: a wrong number we can fix, a missing one we cannot see.

Constructs: EXPOSURE = share of what people actually consumed (visits, time, impressions) that was misinformation; REACH = share of people who encountered it at least once, from behavioural data; SHARING = share of shares, retweets or posts that carried misinformation, or share of users who shared it, from platform data; CONCENTRATION = share of all misinformation exposure or sharing accounted for by a top group of users or sources; RECALL = share of survey respondents who say they saw or shared it; CONTENT = share of items in a sample classified as misinformation; QUALITY = a quality or reliability rating, not a truth judgement; OTHER.

Order: papers are grouped in three tiers. Tier 1 (40 papers, 114 estimates) carries the paper's behavioural claims and comes first; tier 2 (23, 50) adds sharing; tier 3 (26, 57) adds surveys. Stop wherever your time runs out; every completed paper counts. Answers save automatically in this browser; download the CSV when you stop.
