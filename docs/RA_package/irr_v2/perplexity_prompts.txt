You are helping code data for a systematic review of how much misinformation people are exposed to. For each numbered item below you are given a verbatim quote from a research paper and how that paper defined misinformation.

Classify each item on three dimensions. The governing rule: THE CONSTRUCT IS SET BY THE DENOMINATOR -- by what the percentage is a share OF -- not by the topic and not by what is being counted on top. Ask yourself: if this percentage were 100%, what would that mean?

CONSTRUCT (choose exactly one):
- CONTENT = share of items that exist in some corpus (posts, videos, articles, search results)
- EXPOSURE = share of what people actually consumed (views, visits, clicks, time, news diet)
- REACH = share of PEOPLE who encountered it at least once
- SHARING = share of what was shared, retweeted, liked or engaged with
- RECALL = share of people who SAID in a survey they had seen it
- CONCENTRATION = activity attributable to a top slice of users or sources
- QUALITY = a quality/usefulness rating rather than truth or falsity
- OTHER = none of the above

DENOMINATOR CLASS (choose exactly one):
population | all_media | political_news | topical | curated_sample | single_source

BREADTH -- how wide a net the paper cast in deciding what counts as misinformation:
fabricated | false | misleading | unreliable_source | low_quality

Notes that matter:
- If a paper sampled the "most-shared" posts and then coded how many are false, the denominator is the POSTS -- that is CONTENT, not SHARING. Sharing was only the sampling method.
- If the measure is about usefulness, completeness or guideline-adherence rather than truth, it is QUALITY. Test: could the item be accurate but unhelpful?
- Visiting untrustworthy websites is EXPOSURE even though falsity was judged by domain.
- If the denominator is itself made of misinformation (e.g. "of the false claims we collected, 30% were about vaccines"), that is OTHER -- it is composition within misinformation, not prevalence.

Answer with ONE CSV line per item and nothing else. Format:
item_id,construct,denominator_class,breadth,confidence_1to5
