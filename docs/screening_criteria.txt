SCREENING CRITERIA — Stage 1 (title) — systematic review on the PREVALENCE and
CONCENTRATION of misinformation exposure.

"Misinformation" is broad: misinformation, disinformation, fake/false news,
unreliable/untrustworthy websites or sources, junk/low-quality news.

Assign one label per title, using your own judgment (do NOT keyword-match):

INCLUDE — the title clearly indicates a quantitative estimate of one of:
  - audience EXPOSURE to misinformation (share of diet, reach, impressions, views);
  - CONSUMPTION of misinformation (visits, time, views);
  - observed real-world SHARING / spread / dissemination of misinformation;
  - CONCENTRATION of any of the above (share by top X%, supersharers, Gini);
  - actor-level prevalence (misinformation as a share of politicians'/outlets'/
    influencers' output);
  - CONTENT prevalence (share of posts/items that are false/unreliable).

MAYBE — plausibly one of the above but the title is ambiguous; OR you are unsure.
  >>> When in doubt, choose MAYBE. Never EXCLUDE a possibly-relevant study. <<<

EXCLUDE — only if the title is CLEARLY one of:
  - computer-science DETECTION / classification / algorithm / model method;
  - intervention / correction / fact-check-effect / inoculation / "nudge" study;
  - belief / susceptibility / perception-only (who believes), no exposure estimate;
  - HYPOTHETICAL or stated sharing INTENTIONS / lab "would you share" experiments;
  - non-empirical: commentary, theory, book, editorial, proceedings volume,
    dataset-resource paper;
  - clearly off-topic (unrelated clinical / marketing / education / library / film /
    engineering etc.).

Output for each record: label (INCLUDE/MAYBE/EXCLUDE) + a reason of <= 12 words.
