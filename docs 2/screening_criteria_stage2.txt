SCREENING CRITERIA — Stage 2 (ABSTRACT) — systematic review on the PREVALENCE and
CONCENTRATION of misinformation exposure.

You now have the ABSTRACT (not just the title), so you can be DECISIVE: confirm whether
the study actually REPORTS a quantitative estimate, rather than just plausibly might.

"Misinformation" is broad: misinformation, disinformation, fake/false news,
unreliable/untrustworthy websites or sources, junk/low-quality news.

Assign one label per record, judging from title + abstract:

INCLUDE — the abstract indicates the study REPORTS (or clearly will report) a
QUANTITATIVE estimate of at least one of:
  - audience EXPOSURE to misinformation (share of diet, reach, impressions, views);
  - CONSUMPTION of misinformation (visits, time, views);
  - observed real-world SHARING / spread / dissemination of misinformation;
  - CONCENTRATION of any of the above (share by top X%, supersharers, Gini);
  - actor-level prevalence (misinformation as a share of politicians'/outlets'/
    influencers' output);
  - CONTENT prevalence (share of posts/items that are false/unreliable) — INCLUDE but
    note it is content-level (separate bucket).
  Require a population/denominator and an empirical, quantitative design.

EXCLUDE — the abstract shows the study is actually one of:
  - computer-science DETECTION/classification/model/algorithm method (performance, not
    human prevalence);
  - intervention/correction/fact-check-effect/inoculation/nudge study;
  - belief/susceptibility/perception-only (who believes), no exposure/consumption/
    sharing estimate;
  - HYPOTHETICAL or stated sharing INTENTIONS / lab "would you share" experiments
    (we want OBSERVED real-world behaviour, not hypothetical);
  - non-empirical (commentary, theory, review, editorial);
  - off-topic, or "misinformation" used only rhetorically with no measurement.

MAYBE — abstract is MISSING/empty, or genuinely ambiguous about whether a quantitative
prevalence/exposure/concentration estimate is reported. These go to full-text screening.
Do NOT EXCLUDE a record merely because its abstract is missing — label it MAYBE.

Output per record: label (INCLUDE/MAYBE/EXCLUDE) + a short reason (<=15 words) citing
the abstract evidence (e.g. "reports 1.18% of political exposure to fake-news domains").
